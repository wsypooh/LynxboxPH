You can invite people to work in your account from the **Team** page. The number of seats depends on your plan.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including billing, deleting records, and managing the team |
| Manager | Create and edit records; cannot do owner-only actions such as deleting or billing |
| Staff | Create and edit records |
| Viewer | Read-only |

## Inviting someone

1. Open **Team** and choose **Invite**.
2. Enter their email and role.
3. They receive an email with a temporary password and sign in.

A role change takes effect on their next action.

## Working in more than one account

If you belong to several accounts, use the account switcher to change which one you are working in. The page reloads after switching.

## Subscription past due

If your subscription becomes past due, your account becomes read-limited until the owner pays. See [Billing and Subscription](/help/billing-and-subscription).
