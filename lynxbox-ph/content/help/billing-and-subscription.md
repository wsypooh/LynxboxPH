Only the account **owner** can manage billing.

## Free trial

From **Billing**, start a 30-day trial of a paid plan. No credit card is needed. We send a reminder before the trial ends. If you do not pay, the account returns to the Free plan.

## Paying

1. Choose a plan and billing cycle on the Billing page.
2. Pay by **GCash** or **bank transfer**.
3. Submit your payment proof (a screenshot or receipt) with the reference details.
4. A platform administrator verifies it and activates or extends your plan.

Your submissions are listed under **Payment History**.

## Promo codes

Enter a promo code on the payment form to preview the discount. Codes are for new customers, one code at a time, and may apply for a limited duration.

## Renewing

You get a reminder about 5 days before your period ends. Renewing the same plan and cycle before it ends **adds** to your current period instead of resetting it. Changing plan or cycle starts a new period from approval, with no proration.

## If a payment is late

When an active period ends without payment, the account becomes **past due** and is limited to read-only use right away. Paying restores access. After 14 days past due, the account reverts to the Free plan; listings above the Free limit are unlisted, not deleted.

## Downgrading

You can downgrade to Free yourself from the Billing page.
